Every call, above board

Built honest,
from the first ring.

RingAnvil is a receptionist, never a technician — and it never pretends to be a person. The rules below are designed into every account, not added on request.

Disclosed, every call

Every call opens with a recorded-call disclosure, in every state. If a caller asks, the assistant says plainly that it is an automated assistant working for your business.

Emergencies move fast

Gas smell, active flooding, sparking, no heat with vulnerable occupants — scripted urgent categories route straight to your on-call number, and danger to life is directed to 911 first. The AI never tries to handle a life-safety emergency itself.

Follow-up with permission

Follow-up calls and texts go only to people who contacted your business first. STOP is honoured instantly and permanently, quiet hours are respected, and we never place cold outbound calls. Ever.
